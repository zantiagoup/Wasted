What it is made of

Polyethylene terephthalate, resin code 1. It is accepted by every collection route in this locality as long as it is clean and dry.

Before you throw it

  • Empty it and rinse it once. Any residue sends the whole batch to landfill.
  • Take the cap off and keep it. Caps are a different plastic and are collected separately.
  • Crush it lengthwise so it does not take four times the space it needs.

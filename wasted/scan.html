Four steps, and you can stop at any of them.

  1. You take the photo Or you pick one you already have. No camera? The text entry gets you to the same answer.
  2. Wasted reads the image The photo goes to the vision model, which must answer in a fixed JSON schema: object, material, category, preparation.
  3. The category is matched to a drop-off point Wasted compares the material against what each authorised site in your locality accepts.
  4. You confirm or correct Every answer carries a confidence score. When it is wrong, saying so is one tap.

The photo is discarded as soon as the answer comes back. Nothing about it is written to disk.

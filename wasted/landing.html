How it works.

  1. Take a photo of whatever you are about to throw away.

  2. We check what it is made of and how to get it ready.

  3. We show you the closest authorised place that takes it.

  4. Confirm and earn a new badge every ten items.

Throw it right.

Wasted runs in your phone's browser. Nothing to install, and we never keep your photos.
